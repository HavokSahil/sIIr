A music observatory for the curious.

Analyze live microphone input or local audio files with spectrograms, native ECQT pitch classes, beat tracking, spectral descriptors, correlation and PCA. Follow playback, zoom into fullscreen plots and mark moments.

Visualize music with colorful, touch-reactive ink. Generate music from cellular automata: edit seeds, rules, scales and tempo, then play, loop or export WAV.

Maths panels explain the intuition and formulas behind the tools. Audio and analysis stay on your device.

Fluid motion remains experimental. Beat tracking uses spectral flux; calibrated zero device latency is not claimed.
